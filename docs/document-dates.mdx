title: "Document dates"
description: "Dates linked to document text, automatic application, review-first mode, and Calendar."
---

Suchi can find important dates in document text and place them on Calendar. Each
date keeps its document and the exact matching text, so you can check the result
instead of trusting an unexplained event.

New dates that meet the model-confidence threshold reach Calendar automatically
by default. Choose **Review first** under **Application mode** to check inferred
changes in Approvals. Existing accepted and rejected dates retain their recorded
history.

Calendar, review queues, counts, date queries and local ICS export use the
captured filing system and visible documents. Membership and document ACLs
remain required; an approval assignment does not grant entry. Switching systems
clears the former Calendar/review context. Date/model policy settings remain
instance-wide.

## How it works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    D[New or rescanned document] --> M[Model suggests dates]
    M --> V[Check date and document text]
    V -->|Apply when confident and date meets threshold| K[Calendar]
    V -->|Review first or below threshold| A[Approvals]
    V -->|Missing or contradictory text| U[Discard result]
    A -->|Add to Calendar| K[Calendar]
    A -->|Reject| R[Removed from review]
```

For each document, the existing classification request may suggest up to three
important dates. Suchi checks the date, its role, and the matching text before
storing it. This does not add a second model request.

The model chooses a role from the document's context. These are the eight
allowed roles and their intended meanings, not hard-coded classification rules:

| Role      | Intended meaning                                    |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `issued`  | When a document was issued                          |
| `due`     | A payment or action deadline                        |
| `start`   | When an agreement, coverage, or activity begins     |
| `end`     | When a period or activity ends                      |
| `expiry`  | When something stops being valid                    |
| `renewal` | When an agreement or subscription renews            |
| `service` | When a service was provided                         |
| `other`   | Another relevant date that does not fit those roles |

Suchi checks the date format, allowed role, exact quote, and matching date
spelling in the original extracted text. The quoted date must contain exactly
the day, month and year the precision names, in any order: numbers, or a month
name or common abbreviation in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian,
Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish, Czech, Turkish,
Russian, Ukrainian or Thai. `2026年10月13日`, `2026년 10월 13일`, Japanese era
years (`令和8年`), Buddhist-era years, two-digit years and full-width, Arabic,
Persian, Devanagari, Bengali or Thai digits are read as numbers. An extra number
(such as a time), a second month name, or a month that disagrees rejects the
date rather than guessing. These checks do not independently
verify the model's interpretation of a role. Read the quote and open the
document before relying on a date; even a model-confidence score of 100% is not
a guarantee.

## Date precision and model confidence

Precision describes how much of the date the document supplies. There are
exactly three values:

| Precision | Meaning                              | Example display |
| --------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------- |
| `day`     | A specific day                       | 6 Aug 2026      |
| `month`   | A month, without a known day         | Aug 2026        |
| `year`    | A year, without a known month or day | 2026            |

Calendar and Approvals display only the known precision. For storage and
sorting, August 2026 uses `2026-08-01` and the year 2026 uses `2026-01-01`.
Those first-day placeholders do not mean the document names that exact day.

Model confidence is the model's self-reported score for its suggestion, not a
measured probability of correctness. A model can report high confidence even
when the document names only a year. Percentages are rounded; even **100%** is
not a guarantee. Check the document text before relying on a date.

## Configure application mode

Open **Settings → Archive configuration → Classification**.

**Apply when confident** is selected by default:

- **Apply when confident:** Add dates to Calendar and apply suggested metadata
  when they meet your thresholds.
- **Review first:** Send inferred changes to Approvals before they apply.

Use **Save application mode** to save this choice independently of matching,
model activation, credentials, and reading-depth settings. It covers new
inferred dates, model metadata, and local archive matching, not explicit rules
written by a user.

The model/date threshold defaults to `0.70`. Each date uses its own score,
independently of the document's overall classification score. Local matching
needs no model: its automatic threshold defaults to `0.90`, with a `0.50`
minimum review-suggestion score. Below-threshold usable dates still need review.

Configure the optional model and choose **Test connection**. The test uses
synthetic text; it checks connectivity and response format, not accuracy, and
does not enable the model. **Enable model and save options** activates the tested
configuration. Application mode does not authorize external text sharing.

Saving settings, lowering a threshold or restarting Suchi never promotes existing
pending dates. The current mode applies to new ingests and explicit rescans.

## Automatic and reviewed dates

Calendar distinguishes how each date got there:

- An **automatic** date met the current threshold policy and was accepted
  without a human reviewer or review time. The exact document text is still
  checked.
- A **previously accepted** date has no recorded review. This includes dates
  accepted automatically by older versions. Its value, original text, and
  attribution are preserved; Suchi does not invent a reviewer.
- A **reviewed** date records the person's decision and review time. Rejected
  dates do not appear on Calendar.

Calendar leaves automatic dates unlabeled. Previously accepted dates keep a
manila chip and reviewed dates a green one. **From the document** holds matching
text and **Model confidence**. Automatic still means accepted without review
under `threshold-auto-v1`, not merely a missing reviewer.

Rescanning replaces pending model suggestions, not accepted or rejected dates.
New suggestion IDs are never reused. Older pending suggestions without a
provable document version cannot be accepted; request fresh extraction instead.
An older immutable document version retains its own review history: a new
version does not retarget an earlier date or copy its authority.

## Review dates in Approvals

Approvals lists dates that still need a decision under **Dates for Calendar**,
grouped by document. When there are more than two document groups, desktop uses
a two-column grid; mobile remains a single column.

For each date, read the suggested date, role, exact document text and **How
this was suggested** details, then choose **Add to Calendar** or **Reject**.
When more than one date can be added, **Add all N dates to Calendar** in the
toolbar accepts every one of them at once. Dates you leave alone stay pending.

Stale or unsupported suggestions cannot be added; supported stale suggestions
can still be rejected. Sensitive text requires **Reveal**. Refresh after
document or access changes; queued extraction is not an accepted date.

See [Approvals](/approvals) for permissions and API behavior.

## Find dates on Calendar

The public demo has read-only, curated dates labeled **Demo example**. They are
not live model results and do not show **Model confidence**. Its default
Calendar lists all example dates across months and years. Model calls,
date extraction, and date review remain unavailable to demo visitors.

Calendar pairs a grid with a date-grouped agenda. It defaults to the current
fixed month; **30 Days** and **2 Weeks** select rolling ranges beginning today.
Arrows move one complete month or rolling range, and the current-month control
resets the default. Range, saved View, and date-role state stays in the URL.
The **All documents** scope still enforces document access rules.

Only exact-day dates populate grid cells. Month-only and year-only dates remain
in the agenda when their sorting date falls in the range. Research links omit
range bounds and include all dates.

### Focus a day's dates

Selecting a day highlights its cell and scrolls the adjacent feed to its full
group without changing the URL, filters, or query. A direct exact-day URL instead
opens a paged full-width agenda, for example
`#/calendar?date=2026-08-06&role=due`; **Back to Calendar** returns to the current
month with View and role intact.

### Dates linked from Ask your documents

**Open N calendar dates** opens every accepted date for the answer's documents.
The **Dates from N selected documents · All dates** banner counts documents,
not dates. **Date role** still filters the agenda; range controls and **Document
view** stay hidden because the route already names the documents. Results over
500 use **Previous** and **Next**. **Open full calendar** clears the document
scope and returns to the current fixed month.

### Add a reviewed date to another calendar

In an agenda entry, expand **Add to calendar**, read the export notice, and
choose **Download .ics**. Open or import the file in a calendar application.
This is available for reviewed, accepted dates with an exact day, for any date
role. Automatic dates do not offer export. Neither do month/year-only dates,
whose sorting placeholders are not known calendar days.

The downloaded file contains one all-day event with the document title, date
role, and a link to the document. It contains no attachment or quoted document
text. The link still requires the recipient's normal Suchi access; an export
does not create a public share link.

Suchi generates the file locally in the browser, without connecting a calendar
account. Importing it into a synced calendar may send its contents to that
calendar's provider. The file is a snapshot, not a subscription: later changes
in Suchi do not update an imported event. Repeated exports from the same archive
URL retain the event's identifier, but duplicate-import behavior depends on the
calendar application. No reminder, recurrence, or time zone is inferred, and
the event does not mark the day as busy.

### Search by date

You can also use dates in the [query language](/query-language):

```text
date:2026-08-06
date:>=2026-08-01 date:<2026-09-01
date-role:renewal
is:dated
```

Pending and rejected dates never match these filters.

## Apply the current settings to older documents

Model settings affect new documents and explicitly requested extraction.
They never change the decision on a pending, accepted or rejected date.

To process older documents:

1. Open **Documents**.
2. Select the documents.
3. Choose **Rescan** or **Extract dates**.

New usable results follow the current mode and their own model-confidence score.
Rescanning preserves accepted and rejected dates, including older automatic
acceptance. Automatic acceptance and review check the current document version
and exact text inside the database transaction; in-flight human decisions are
not overwritten. Review also checks current document access. Retrying the same
recorded decision does not apply it twice; an opposite decision on an already
resolved suggestion is a conflict.

See [Pipeline versions and rescans](/pipeline-versions) for the difference
between unprocessed, stale, and completed documents.

## Why a date might be missing

Check these in order:

1. **Approvals:** the date may be waiting for a decision.
2. **Calendar filters:** check the date role and exact documents. On the full
   calendar, also check the month and Document view.
3. **Document text:** open the document and confirm extraction captured the date.
4. **Model settings:** confirm the model is active and check application mode
   and threshold.
5. **Processing:** select the document and run **Rescan** or **Extract dates**.
6. **Access:** confirm you can read the document and have **Manage document
   dates** permission.

If no valid date appears after a successful rescan, inspect the processing job
under Approvals and the model configuration under
[LLM classifier](/llm-classifier).

## Current limits

- Dates are all-day document facts, not timed meetings.
- Time zones and reminders are not inferred.
- Relative phrases that cannot be normalized from explicit document text are
  ignored.
- Bare account numbers, totals, and version numbers are not treated as dates.
- At most three dates are proposed per classification run.
- Existing Calendar dates are never silently removed by a setting change.

For the storage fields and HTTP contracts, see [Ask your documents](/archive-chat)
and [API reference](/api#extracted-facts-archive_intelligence).
